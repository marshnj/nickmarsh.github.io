Sol Information:


Sol is a G-type star with a subclassification of GV2. It is approximately 4.6 billion years old and is about halfway through its life on the main sequence. Sol orbits the Milky Way galaxy at a distance between 24,000 to 28,000 light years from the center. The orbital period is 225 to 250 million years; Sol has only orbited the galaxy about eighteen times so far. Sol is brighter than 85% of the stars in the Milky Way galaxy. The solar system is considered very unusual.


Based on observations of extrasolar planets, only one other known system, Kepler-90, has eight planets. Most observed systems have a much lower planet count. Only 10% of stars have a planet as large as Jupiter. Over 50% of stars are in multiple star systems. Only 7.6% of the stars in the Milky Way are G-type. Most other observed systems have large planets closer to the star. The solar system has only small, rocky planets close to Sol. The solar system is also extremely rich in rare elements like Gold and Uranium. So far as we know, Sol is also the only star with a life bearing, inhabited planet.
