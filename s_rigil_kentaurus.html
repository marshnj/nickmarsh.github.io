Rigel Kentaurus Information:


Rigel Kentaurus is the largest, brightest member of the three-star Alpha Centauri system. It is the same stellar classification as Sol (G2V); however, it is slightly larger and brighter than Sol. The distance between Rigel Kentaurus and Sol is about 4.37 light years. The name Rigel Kentaurus comes from the Arabic phrase "Rijl Qanṭūris", meaning "foot of the Centaur". This is because it is part of the constellation known as the Centaur.


Rigel Kentaurus does not have any confirmed exoplanets. The 80 year highly elliptical orbit of Rigel Kentaurus and Toliman varies between the distance between Saturn and Sol to about the distance between Pluto and Sol. It has been suggested that the orbits of two stars so close to each other may have prevented planets from forming, or caused the orbits of any planets that did form to be disturbed so they fell into one of the stars or were ejected from the system.
