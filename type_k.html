K-type Star Characteristics:


K-type stars, often called orange dwarfs, are medium-temperature, main-sequence stars with surface temperatures between 3,900 and 5,200 Kelvin. They are more stable than M-type stars and are considered good candidates for hosting habitable planets due to their long lifespans and stable output. These stars are cooler and dimmer than our Sun but more luminous than M-type stars.

Toliman (Alpha Centauri B)


Toliman, also known as Alpha Centauri B, is part of the Alpha Centauri triple star system and is approximately 4.37 light-years from Earth. It is a stable K-type main-sequence star and a potential target in the search for habitable exoplanets.
