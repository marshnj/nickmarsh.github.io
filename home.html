Star Classification

Stars are classified based on temperature, from the hottest (O) to the coolest (M). There are six stars within eight light years of our Sun - two type G, one type K, and three type M. The table shows the seven star types and their temperatures and color. Their mass and radius are shown relative to the size of the Sun (Sol) and also the percentage the type of star is of all stars in the galaxy. Roll over the table to see relative sizes, temperatures, and colors of the seven star types.
