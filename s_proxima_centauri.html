Proxima Centauri Information:


Proxima Centauri is a red dwarf star of spectral type M5.5Ve and is the closest known star to the Sun, located about 4.24 light-years away. It is part of the Alpha Centauri system but is gravitationally bound only loosely to Alpha Centauri A and B.


Though dim and small, Proxima Centauri is of great interest due to its confirmed exoplanets. The most notable is Proxima b, an Earth-sized planet in the habitable zone, discovered in 2016. A second planet, Proxima d, was later confirmed in 2022. These discoveries have made Proxima a major target in the search for life beyond our solar system.
